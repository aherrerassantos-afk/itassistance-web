FAQ

What does an Airbnb guest assistant do?

A guest assistant handles all communication with your guests: messages on Airbnb and Booking.com, 24/7 support calls, emergency management and check-in coordination. itassistance does this for a flat monthly fee — no commission on your revenue.

How is itassistance different from a property manager?

A property manager takes 15–25% of your gross revenue and manages everything end-to-end. itassistance focuses exclusively on guest communication and check-in, at a fixed fee of €55/month — you keep 100% of your revenue.

Do you cover all areas of Florence?

Yes — Historic Centre, Oltrarno, Campo di Marte, Novoli, Gavinana, Rifredi and surrounding areas. We also operate in Tuscany, and across Spain and France for remote support.

How quickly can you start?

Onboarding takes 48 hours. We set up portal access, create a guest welcome guide and activate your dedicated support number.
